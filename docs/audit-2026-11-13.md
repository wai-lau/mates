## Structure

The main costs: `cli.py` (1,385 lines) does eight jobs and other modules import its private
helpers; the check sandbox is set up twice and the copies drift; four tmux wrappers; OpenCode
queries spread over three modules with no seam for a second backend; worker paths
(`mates-qwen-N`) hardcoded in several places; log records built in five places. Proposed
layout: `config.py`, `state.py`, `tmux.py`, `screen.py`, `backend/opencode.py`, `gitwork.py`,
`checks.py`, `commands/` with a registry, and a small `cli.py`.
